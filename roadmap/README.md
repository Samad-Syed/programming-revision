## Learning Rules

1. Work through the roadmap progressively.
2. Solve a representative selection of problems rather than every problem.
3. Advance when the core concepts are understood and can be applied independently.
4. Track unfinished problems and weak areas for future revision.
5. Record completed levels and next steps in the repository's `PROGRESS.md`.

## Progress Tracking

The repository's root `PROGRESS.md` is the source of truth for current progress. The PDFs are reference material; language-specific directories contain implementations and solutions.
